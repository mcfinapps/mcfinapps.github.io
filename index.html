Privacy is a product decision

Your data is not
our raw material.

McFin apps do not build advertising profiles, run behavioral analytics or sell personal data. Core processing and personal history stay on your device whenever the product allows it.

If an app genuinely needs a connection, it serves the feature—not a server-side dossier. We make software and sell it to you. A suspiciously traditional arrangement.
