A scan you can correct

Help

Overplan · Last updated 5 August 2026

Overplan turns a room into a plan you can correct. Scan it if your device has LiDAR, or draw it by hand if it does not — then fix what the scanner got wrong, which is the part most scanning apps leave you stuck with.

Starting a plan

Scan a room uses your device's LiDAR camera. It needs camera access, and it appears on iPhone and iPad models that have a LiDAR sensor. Sketch a floorplan works on every device: tap to place corners, close the loop, and the walls stand up.

If Scan a room tells you the device is not supported, sketching is the way in — it is the same editor and the same plan afterwards.

Correcting the plan

Tap a wall to select it, then drag to move it. Doors and windows are added from Add — choose what you want first, set its size, then tap the wall where it goes. Furniture works the same way, from the library.

A scan is a starting point, not a verdict. Walls can be moved, openings repositioned, and the room's height adjusted after the fact.

Measuring

Measure snaps to real features — a corner, an edge, a wall face — and names what it snapped to, so a dimension is never taken from a point that does not exist. Wall-to-wall gives the clear dimension between finished faces, which is the number you would get with a tape.

Sharing

Export a to-scale PDF drawing for anyone who needs to print or mark it up, or a 3D model (USDZ, OBJ or GLB) for anyone with 3D tools. Nothing leaves your device until you choose to share it.

Your plans

Every plan is kept on your device, with the original scan preserved separately from your edits — so Restore Original Scan can always take you back to what the scanner actually measured.

Troubleshooting

Scanning shows a black screen or nothing happens. Camera access is off. Open iOS Settings → Overplan → Camera, or use the Open Settings button in the app's own alert.

The room looks flat when it first opens. Shading is computed after the plan appears and settles a moment later. This is normal.

A measurement looks wrong. Check which feature it snapped to — the label says. Dimensions are taken between wall faces, not centre lines.
