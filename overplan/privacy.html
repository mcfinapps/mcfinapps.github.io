Privacy by architecture

Privacy Policy

Overplan · Last updated 30 July 2026

Your rooms stay yours.

Overplan collects no personal data. There is no account, no McFin server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests and works offline.

Camera, LiDAR and RoomPlan

Overplan uses your device’s camera, motion sensors and LiDAR scanner through Apple’s ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks to understand the room you choose to scan.

  • Camera and depth data are processed on your device. Overplan does not upload the camera view, video, photographs or depth stream.
  • The resulting room geometry is saved locally. This can include walls, floors, doors, windows, dimensions, measurements and edits you make.
  • The camera is used only while scanning. Overplan asks for camera permission because RoomPlan requires it; the app has no microphone or location purpose.

Scanning, editing, measuring and exporting work without an internet connection.

Saved plans

Your Overplan projects are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. McFin cannot see or retrieve them. They remain there until you delete the plan or remove the app.

Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to the backup settings you control. Those backups are provided and governed by Apple, not McFin.

PDF and 3D sharing

Overplan can create floor-plan PDFs and 3D files such as USDZ, GLB and OBJ. These files are generated on your device and can reveal the layout, dimensions and openings of the space you scanned.

No export happens automatically. A file leaves Overplan only when you choose Share and select a person, app, device or storage service in Apple’s standard sharing interface. After you share it, the recipient or selected service handles that copy under its own privacy practices.

What we collect

Nothing. Overplan has no account system, cloud database, analytics service, advertising SDK, tracking SDK or third-party crash-reporting service. Because McFin receives no app data, there is no personal data held by us to access, sell or delete.

If you have separately enabled Apple’s option to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may provide limited diagnostic information under Apple’s own privacy terms. Overplan does not transmit those diagnostics itself.

Children

Overplan does not knowingly collect information from children—or from anyone else. It requires no account and gathers no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy will be posted here with a new date. Any future feature that materially changes how data is handled will be explained before it is used.

Contact
