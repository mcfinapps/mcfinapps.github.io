Real Overplan interface · no generated UI

01 · Acquire

Scan it.
Sketch it or trace it.

Use LiDAR for a fast measured starting point, draw freely, or trace over an existing image or PDF plan. Every route leads to the same clean, editable model.

02 · Clean result

The room as space.
Not as stuff.

Overplan keeps the architecture in focus. The uncluttered cream-clay view makes rooms, walls and openings immediately legible—without recreating everything that happened to be in the room.

Clean by default · editable throughout · architecture in focus

03 · Refine

Shape it until it’s right.

Add new walls, doors and windows whenever the plan needs them. Tap any element to move it, resize it or reveal the dimensions and actions that belong to it. Nothing more.

04 · Measure

Measure what matters.

Snap dimensions to corners, edges, faces or finished wall surfaces. Saved measurements stay with the plan and follow the architecture as it changes.

05 · Share

A plan you can actually use.

Keep or share the complete .overplan document as a fully editable backup, or move it to another iPhone or iPad and continue working. For finished output, choose an honest PDF print scale or send a USDZ that opens directly in Apple’s AR viewer—even without Overplan installed.
